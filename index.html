Applications & Guidelines

Artists

Page artists will be expected to create either a single page, a 2 page spread, or a comic.

  • Submit at least 3 samples of your best work.
    At least one must include a background and one One Piece sample.
  • The style in your samples should match the style you intend to use for your zine piece.
  • Samples must be fully completed.
    Please do not include WIPs.
  • NSFW samples are allowed, but please tag them accordingly.
  • A portfolio may be submitted.
  • Ensure all links are accessible.
One Piece pieces are encouraged but not mandatory.

Merch Artists

Merch artists will be expected to create 1-3 pieces of digital merch for the zine.

  • Submit at least 3 samples of your best work (digital or physical merch), one of which must be One Piece merch.
  • The style in your samples should be the one you intend on utilising in your zine piece.
  • Samples must be fully completed; please do not include WIPs.
  • NSFW samples are allowed, but please tag these accordingly.
  • A portfolio may be submitted.
  • Ensure all links are accessible.
One Piece pieces are encouraged but not mandatory.

Writers

Writers will be expected to create a fic between 2.5-3k words.

  • Please submit 3 samples of your best work, one of which must be a One Piece fic.
  • Samples should be 1.5–2k words long; you may include excerpts from longer fics.
  • NSFW samples are allowed, but please tag these accordingly.
  • A portfolio may be submitted.
  • Ensure all links are accessible.
One Piece pieces are encouraged but not mandatory.

Spot Artists

Spot artists will be creating 2-3 illustrations to accompany a writer’s piece.

  • Please submit at least 3 samples of your best work, one of which must be a One Piece sample. Examples of prior spot art are preferred.
  • The style in your samples should be the one you intend on utilising in your zine piece.
  • Samples must be fully completed; please do not include WIPs.
  • NSFW samples are allowed, but please tag these accordingly.
  • A portfolio may be submitted.
  • Ensure all links are accessible.
One Piece pieces are encouraged but not mandatory.

FAQ

What is a fanzine? +

A zine is a booklet that collects art, writing or other creative materials, they can be made by anyone and the sky's the limit on what your zine could be about. Zines have a long history with fanzines in particular dating back to the 1960s in the Star Trek fandom.

Will the zine be application or sign up based? +

The zine will be sign up based, the number of contributors that will be accepted will be decided based on the results of the interest check.

Will the zine be spoiler free? +

Unfortunately, no.

Will AUs be included in the zine? +

This will depend on the results of the interest check.

Are headcanons allowed? +

Yes.
